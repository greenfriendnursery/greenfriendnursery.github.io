Shipping & delivery

Live plants, packed carefully, sent from our gardens in Malda and Murshidabad to every serviceable PIN code in India.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1 Where we deliver

We ship across India through our courier partners. Enter your 6-digit PIN code in the Check delivery box on the home page or on any product page to see whether your area is serviceable, which courier will carry the parcel, and the exact delivery charge before you order.

If your PIN code is not serviceable today, it may open later — courier networks change often. You are welcome to ask us to check again.

2 Delivery time

Delivery areaUsual time after dispatch
Malda & Murshidabad2–3 working days
Rest of West Bengal4–5 working days
Other states5–7 working days

These are usual times, not a guarantee. Weather, festivals, strikes and courier delays can add a day or two. Tracking details are sent as soon as the parcel leaves us.

3 Delivery charges

  • Free delivery on every order of ₹1000 or more.
  • Below ₹1000, delivery starts from ₹150. The final amount depends on your PIN code, the courier and the weight of the parcel.
  • The charge is always shown in the cart and at checkout before you pay. There are no hidden fees.
  • Multi-seller orders may travel as separate parcels, because each seller dispatches from their own nursery. You still pay one delivery charge at checkout.
Planting a full garden? Adding one more plant often crosses ₹1000 and makes delivery free — the cart tells you exactly how much more is needed.

4 Packing live plants

Roots are wrapped in moist coco-peat, the stem is supported, and the pot is cushioned inside a sturdy carton. Leaves may look slightly tired on arrival after a long journey — that is normal. Give the plant water, light and a few days, and it recovers.

Please open the parcel within 24 hours of delivery so any transit damage can be reported in time. See the replacement policy.

5 Tracking your parcel

When your order is dispatched you receive the courier name and tracking number. You can also see the status of every order in your account on this website.

If tracking has not moved for more than 48 hours, write to us at mobasserhoque@gmail.com with your order number and we will follow it up with the courier.

6 Address changes and failed delivery

Tell us as early as possible if the address or PIN code needs correcting — once a parcel is handed to the courier, changes may not be possible.

If a delivery fails because the address is incomplete, nobody is available, or the parcel is refused, the courier may return it to us. In that case the delivery charge is not refundable, and a second dispatch is treated as a new order.

7 Payment on delivery

Cash on delivery, UPI (with the 12-digit UTR) and bank transfer are all accepted. Please keep the exact amount ready where cash on delivery is used.
