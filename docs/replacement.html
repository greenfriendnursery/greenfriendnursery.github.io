Replacement policy

If a plant arrives damaged, dead or different from what you ordered, we replace it free of cost. That is our promise on every order.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1 What is covered

  • A plant that is broken or badly damaged during transit.
  • A plant that is dead on arrival — dry, rotten or with no live root.
  • A wrong plant or wrong variety sent by mistake.
  • A plant that is clearly smaller than the size you paid for.
A few yellow or wilted leaves after a long journey are normal and are not a defect. Water the plant, keep it in shade for two days and it usually revives.

2 How to claim a replacement

  1. Report within 24 hours of delivery. Claims after 24 hours are hard to verify, so please do not wait.
  2. Take a clear photo or short video of the plant and the parcel, showing the damage. A video of the plant still inside the opened box is best.
  3. Send it to us on WhatsApp or email with your order number.
  4. We check the photos and confirm within 1–2 working days.

Once confirmed, the replacement is dispatched on the next available courier cycle. We cover the cost of the replacement and of sending it.

3 What is not covered

  • Damage caused after delivery — overwatering, underwatering, heat, frost, pets or repotting.
  • Normal natural variation: leaf colour, small marks, a bent leaf, a plant that is taller or shorter than the photograph.
  • Claims made more than 24 hours after delivery.
  • A wrong PIN code or incomplete address given at checkout.
  • Plants that were repotted or pruned before the claim was checked.
  • Disease or pests that appear several days after delivery, which usually come from the surroundings.

4 If a replacement is not possible

Sometimes the same variety is out of stock. In that case you may choose:

  • a different plant of equal or lower value, or
  • a full refund of the plant price to your UPI or bank account.

Refunds are usually completed within 5–7 working days of confirmation.

5 Marketplace orders

Some plants on this website are sold by independent sellers. The same replacement guarantee applies. We coordinate with the seller so you do not have to deal with two parties — simply report the problem to us with the order number.

6 Cancellations

An order can be cancelled free of charge before it is dispatched. Once a live plant has left the nursery, cancellation is not possible because plants cannot be resold — but the replacement guarantee still applies if something goes wrong in transit.

7 Speak to a person

Email mobasserhoque@gmail.com with your order number, or message us on WhatsApp using the green button on the shop. We are available 9 am – 9 pm.
